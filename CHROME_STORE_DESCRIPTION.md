## Short Description (132 characters max)
Smart engagement badges show when it's the best time to reply. See follower counts and engagement opportunities at a glance.

## Detailed Description

EngageX is a Twitter engagement helper that displays smart badges next to usernames on your Twitter/X homepage, showing follower counts and highlighting the best opportunities to get your replies noticed.

The extension displays follower counts instantly next to every username and uses color-coded badges to indicate optimal engagement timing: green "Reply now!" badges for high-opportunity moments (big accounts with few replies and fresh tweets), yellow "Good timing" badges for good engagement windows, and standard follower count displays when no special opportunity is detected.

Hovering over any badge reveals detailed stats including follower ratio, reply count, view count, tweet age, and engagement opportunity score. EngageX calculates engagement scores based on follower count, reply count, tweet freshness, and view-to-reply ratio, and works dynamically as you scroll through your timeline, capturing data from regular tweets, retweets, quote tweets, and conversation threads.

This extension works on the Twitter/X homepage only (For You and Following tabs) and does not work on user profiles, tweet detail pages, search results, or other Twitter pages. EngageX only reads data from Twitter's public API endpoints that your browser already loads—no data is sent to external servers and everything stays local in your browser.

Perfect for content creators, marketers, and anyone looking to maximize their engagement on Twitter/X.
